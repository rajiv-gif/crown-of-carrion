# Art Generation Prompts — Chapters III–VII

Paste-ready prompts for the image tool. Two shared style prefixes keep
everything consistent with the existing art.

**UNIT PREFIX** (put before every character prompt):
> Full-body dark fantasy character, grim realistic painted style, muted
> desaturated palette with cold shadows, standing pose facing slightly left,
> full figure visible head to feet, on a solid bright green screen background
> (#00d400), no cropping, single character only

**MAP PREFIX** (put before every background prompt):
> Dark fantasy battlefield background, wide landscape 16:9, grim painted
> style, muted desaturated palette, heavy atmosphere, fog, no characters,
> ground plane open in the middle distance for a tactical grid

## Chapter III — The Bone Congregation

rags, carrying a wide wooden serving board stacked with unidentifiable

### The Warden of the First Table (miniboss)

## Chapter V — The Hollow Choir

### Letter-Husk
UNIT PREFIX + standing human husk wrapped head to toe in vellum strips
covered in a single repeated dead alphabet character, no visible face, ink
bleeding through the wrappings, posture of a person politely waiting

### Name-Eater
UNIT PREFIX + tall faceless monastic figure, habit of woven paper, mouth
region a smooth blank, dozens of tiny stolen mouths whispering from the
sleeves and hem, long fingers stained with ink to the elbow

### Skin Choralist
UNIT PREFIX + choir monk whose face is a stretched borrowed face tied
behind the skull with cord, choir robes, hymnal chained open across the
chest with pages of names crossed out, seams visible at the jawline

### The Choir Beneath the Skin (chapter boss)
UNIT PREFIX + a congregation-shaped mass of overlapping translucent human
silhouettes occupying one robed body, dozens of faint faces at different
depths, none aligned, single choir robe stretched over the multitude, no
ground contact, hem dissolving into whispering script

## Map Backgrounds

### The First Table (III·2)
MAP PREFIX + endless catacomb nave underground, aisle of heaped corpse
offerings down the center, pews built from femur and jawbone, guttering
grave candles, a distant pulpit of fused bone, sickly green-grey light

### The Black Conclave (III·3)
MAP PREFIX + round subterranean conclave chamber, corpse piles ringing the
walls like a pantry, defiled altar on a raised dais at center, one thin
shaft of holy light near the entrance flickering out, oily anointment
stains on the flagstones

### The Weighing (IV·1)
MAP PREFIX + half-drowned norse mead-hall converted to an auction house,
long tables stacked with crated bodies and lot numbers, great iron scale
hanging from the roof beam, cold water over the floor planks, bidding
paddles of bone leaning against benches

### The Oath-Mill (IV·2)
MAP PREFIX + monstrous watermill straddling a black river, millstones
visible through a broken wall grinding pale material, conveyor of biers
feeding the intake, oath-rings hung on hooks along the walls, frost and
spray

### The Scale-Throne (IV·3)
MAP PREFIX + iron throne room where the throne is one pan of a colossal
scale, the counterweight pan heaped with contracts seals and skulls, chain
trenches crossing the floor, cold northern light through arrow slits

### The Scriptorium (V·1)
MAP PREFIX + sunken monastery scriptorium, water to ankle height, lecterns
holding chained books that face the reader, twenty-six alcoves each with a
standing wrapped figure, ink swirling in the floodwater

### The Font (V·2)
MAP PREFIX + perfectly symmetric well-bottom chamber, still black
reflecting pool across the center, mirrored architecture left and right,
pale light from an unseen source above, unsettling exact symmetry

### The Root's Mouth (V·3)
MAP PREFIX + cavern where a colossal dead root descends from the ceiling
into a round pit, concentric stone rings leading inward, script carved on
every surface radiating from the center, breath-like haze rising from the
pit

### The Wheel, Jammed (VI·1)
MAP PREFIX + plane of stacked broken afterlives, colossal jammed wheel of
stone and light seized mid-turn on the horizon, ground littered with the
banners of every prior faction, ash snowing upward

### Ring Battlefields (VI·2 — three variants)
MAP PREFIX + battlefield on an enormous tree ring, dead world-tree bark
rising as a wall on one side, void past the edge, growth-ring grooves
forming natural terrain lines, [variant a: plague-pit relics] [variant b:
rusted oath-chains grown into the wood] [variant c: monastery stones
embedded in the ring]

### The Crownward Throne (VI·3)
MAP PREFIX + summit of the dead world-tree, empty throne of interlocking
black teeth at the far end, seven pedestals in a half circle, the White
Silence visible as a thin tearing membrane in the sky

### The White Silence Dock (VII)
MAP PREFIX + featureless white void with a weathered wooden ferry dock, a
single moored black ferry, seven mooring posts, no horizon line, one
lantern, the suggestion of water without any water
